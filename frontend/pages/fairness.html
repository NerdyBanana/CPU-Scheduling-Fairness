Evaluation Metric: Jain's Fairness Index

Jain's Fairness Index measures how evenly CPU allocation is distributed. Higher values indicate more even allocation. Processes with waiting times exceeding the starvation threshold (\(2 \times \text{Mean Waiting Time}\)) are flagged as starved.
